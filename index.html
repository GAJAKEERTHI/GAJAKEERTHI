About UDILIV 300MG TABLET 15'S

UDILIV 300MG TABLET 15'S (UDCA) is a naturally occurring bile acid used to treat liver disorders and manage gallstones by improving bile flow and offering anti-inflammatory effects.

Uses

  • Aids in dissolving gallstones.
  • Used in primary biliary cholangitis (PBC).
  • Improves liver function and reduces gallstone formation.

How It Works

UDILIV improves bile flow and reduces cholesterol absorption. Dosage varies depending on body weight and specific conditions.

Side Effects

  • Nausea
  • Abdominal discomfort
  • Diarrhea
  • Hair loss (rare)
